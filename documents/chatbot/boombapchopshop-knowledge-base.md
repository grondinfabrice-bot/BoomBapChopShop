## General License Principles

Each beat license covers one final song only.

A final song means a new work that includes the customer's vocal, lyrics, topline, performance, or other original artistic contribution.

Using the instrumental alone without substantial artistic contribution is not allowed.

Using one purchased license for multiple songs is not allowed unless the customer buys additional licenses or receives written approval.

A license gives permission to use the beat under defined terms. It is not a sale of the instrumental and not a full transfer of ownership.

The producer keeps ownership of:

- the instrumental;
- original instrumental master;
- composition and arrangement rights;
- publishing and author rights unless a separate written agreement says otherwise;
- stems, trackouts, source files, presets, samples, and pre-existing elements controlled by the producer.

Recommended credit:
Prod. by BOOM BAP CHOP SHOP

Credit should appear where technically possible, including metadata, DSP credits, YouTube descriptions, SoundCloud, Bandcamp, clips, visualizers, and promotion.
